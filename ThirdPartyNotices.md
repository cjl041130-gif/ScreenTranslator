# Third-party notices

The document translation feature uses the following redistributable libraries.

- **DocumentFormat.OpenXml 3.5.1** — parses, validates, and writes PPTX and DOCX files. License: MIT. Project: https://github.com/dotnet/Open-XML-SDK
- **PdfPig 0.1.16** — reads PDF text, coordinates, and page structure locally. License: Apache-2.0 (with bundled third-party notices). Project: https://github.com/UglyToad/PdfPig
- **PDFsharp-WPF 6.2.4** — creates and modifies PDF files on Windows. License: MIT. Project: https://github.com/empira/PDFsharp

No PDF, PowerPoint, or Word document is sent to these projects or to an online conversion service.
